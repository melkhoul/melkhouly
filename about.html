About

Capability, transformation and the decisions in between.

I’m a learning, talent and capability leader with 15+ years of international experience across Europe, APAC and MENA. My work has evolved from learning experience design and global L&D strategy into analytics, AI-enabled talent development, workforce portfolio strategy and organizational AI adoption.

At Amazon in Luxembourg, I lead the global delivery and expansion of Career Choice Tech programs while driving AI adoption and transformation within Career Choice. Earlier Amazon work included AI-enabled talent-development initiatives such as AI99, GoalMate and HirePulse. Before Amazon, I led global L&D work at Agoda, founded an L&D design consultancy and consulted across MENA.

The common thread is not a particular technology or learning methodology. It is helping organizations make better capability decisions and turn them into changes in how people work and perform.
