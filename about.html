About

Capability, AI and organizational transformation.

I work at the intersection of capability, AI and organizational transformation. My focus is not deploying technology or delivering learning in isolation. It is helping organizations make better capability decisions and translating those decisions into changes in how people work and perform.

I bring 15+ years of international experience across Europe, APAC and MENA. My career has moved from learning design and consulting into global L&D strategy, talent analytics, AI-enabled talent development, workforce portfolio strategy and AI adoption.

Across those roles, I have worked on problems ranging from operating-model redesign and capability strategy to experimentation, analytics, workflow transformation and adoption. The common thread is the level of problem I am interested in: how leaders make better choices about capability, how evidence challenges assumptions, how technology changes workflows, and what conditions allow new ways of working to scale.
