My Story

One question. Six chapters.

I’ve spent 15+ years exploring one question: how do we help people and organizations perform differently? The answer has evolved as the problems became bigger.

Designing better experiences

Learning design & consulting

I learned to look beyond the training request and understand the performance problem underneath it. That thinking became visible in work such as the eLearning Design Map and my early consulting and design practice.

Testing what actually works

Experimentation & impact

I became less interested in whether an intervention looked sophisticated and more interested in whether evidence justified the investment. Experimentation, scrap learning and performance-impact measurement became part of how I made decisions.

Connecting capability to strategy

Global L&D & strategy

The unit of analysis grew from an intervention to the organization: priorities, populations, governance, technology, budget and capability choices. This led to global L&D leadership work and the Learning Strategy Builder.

Using data to challenge assumptions

Talent analytics & performance

Analytics became a leadership tool—not simply a reporting tool—for deciding what to change, what to stop and where to intervene earlier. HirePulse and the HirePro validation study are examples of that shift.

Redesigning work with AI

AI-enabled talent development

Generative AI made it possible to rethink workflows rather than simply digitize existing learning processes. AI99, GoalMate and other AI-enabled decision mechanisms became practical experiments in that possibility.

Building the conditions for transformation

AI adoption & organizational capability

The challenge is now organizational: capability, leadership, governance, infrastructure, behavior and measurable business outcomes. My current work focuses increasingly on the conditions that turn AI access into changed ways of working.

The thread has stayed consistent: diagnose the real problem → challenge assumptions with evidence → build a practical mechanism → enable adoption → measure what changes.
