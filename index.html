My Story

From learning design to AI transformation.

Over 15+ years, the level at which I solve problems has expanded—from individual learning experiences and interventions to capability strategy, decision systems, AI-enabled workflows and the organizational conditions required for transformation.

Designing better experiences

Experience · Learning design & consulting

I started by learning to look beyond the training request. The real work was understanding the performance problem, the learner context and the constraints around it—then designing an experience that addressed the right need. That systems thinking shaped my early consulting and design practice.

Testing what actually works

Evidence · Experimentation & impact

The next shift was from design quality to decision quality. I became less interested in whether an intervention looked sophisticated and more interested in whether evidence justified the investment. Experimentation and performance-impact measurement became mechanisms for deciding what to continue, change or stop.

Connecting capability to strategy

Strategy · Organizational capability

As my scope grew, the unit of analysis moved beyond individual interventions. The question became how to make coherent choices across populations, programs, technology, people, governance and investment—and how to connect those choices to business priorities.

Using data to challenge assumptions

Decisions · Analytics & performance

Analytics became less about reporting what happened and more about improving the decisions that came next. I used evidence to identify where to intervene earlier, test whether established mechanisms were working and challenge assumptions when the data pointed elsewhere.

Redesigning work with AI

Work · AI-enabled transformation

Generative AI created an opportunity to rethink work rather than simply digitize existing processes. My focus shifted to where AI could remove structural friction, improve decision-making and change the workflow itself—not simply add another tool to it.

Building the conditions for transformation

Organization · AI adoption & capability

The challenge is now larger than any single tool or workflow. Sustainable transformation depends on capability, leadership, governance, infrastructure, behavior and measurable business outcomes. My work increasingly focuses on how those conditions come together so that access to AI becomes a different way of working.
