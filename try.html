Frameworks built to be used.

A collection of templates and frameworks I’ve developed while working on learning, capability, evidence and AI transformation. Explore the thinking, then adapt it to your own context.

Strategy framework

Learning Strategy Builder

Turn business priorities into a structured capability strategy by connecting three strategy drivers to nine organizational components.

Three drivers: business goals, annual needs analysis and content maintenance.

Nine components: population, programs, metrics, staffing, technology, capabilities, outsourcing, governance and budget.

This V2 page introduces the framework. A guided, printable builder can be added as the next iteration.

Decision framework

Experimentation in L&D

Structure an experiment so evidence can validate an idea, challenge it—or stop it.

Define the hypothesis, success metric, monitoring metric, do-no-harm measure and explicit decision rule before running the intervention.

The purpose is disciplined decision-making, not experimentation for its own sake.

Design framework

eLearning Design Map

Explore an end-to-end methodology that starts with the performance problem and continues through design, implementation and outcome measurement.

The map connects Data Collection → Validation → Proposal → Project → Impact → Content → Design → Development → Implementation → Outcome.

Its emphasis is on linking learning decisions to context, constraints and measurable outcomes.

Transformation framework

AI Adoption & Transformation

Explore the organizational conditions that help turn AI access into changed behavior, workflows and business outcomes.

The framework looks across five pillars: Adoption, Infrastructure, Governance, Leadership and Capability.

It distinguishes adoption—using AI to improve what we already do—from transformation—rethinking what we should do differently because AI exists.
